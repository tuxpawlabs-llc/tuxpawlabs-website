Privacy and Data Processing Policy

TUXPAW LLC ("TuxPaw", "we") · EU Compliance Kit for Shopify · Version 1.1, September 2026

What we process and why

Merchant data: shop domain, shop contact email, app settings, and plan. Purpose: operating the app and billing through Shopify.

Shopper data (submitted through the withdrawal form): name, email address, and order reference. Purpose: recording the Article 11a withdrawal declaration, matching it to an order in the merchant's store, notifying the merchant, and sending the shopper a timestamped acknowledgement email.

Launch notification requests: if you email us asking to be told when the app becomes available, we process the email address you write from and any shop domain you choose to include. Purpose: sending you that one notification.

We process nothing else about shoppers. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or build profiles. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Legal basis and roles

The merchant is the data controller for shopper data; TuxPaw is a processor acting on the merchant's instructions (see the Data Processing Addendum in our Terms). Processing is necessary to handle the withdrawal declaration the shopper initiates.

Launch notification requests are different: there TuxPaw is the controller, and the legal basis is your consent, given by sending us the request. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us, and we will delete the address.

Retention and deletion

A launch notification address is deleted once that notification has been sent, or within twelve months of your request, whichever comes first. It is never added to a marketing list and never used for any other message.

Withdrawal records are retained for the merchant's audit purposes while the app is installed. We honor all Shopify privacy webhooks: customer data requests, customer redaction (personal fields are erased; anonymised audit counts remain), and full shop redaction after uninstall.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Administrative access is limited to a single TuxPaw operator. The app keeps a timestamped audit record of every action taken on shopper data, including receipt of a withdrawal declaration, the acknowledgement sent, redaction of personal fields, and full deletion on shop redaction. Backups are encrypted.

Subprocessors

Shopify (platform), Railway (application and database hosting, USA), Resend (transactional email, USA), and Zoho (mailbox hosting for our published contact addresses). Each is bound by a data processing agreement incorporating EU Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

Security incidents

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting merchant or shopper data, we will notify affected merchants without undue delay with the information needed for their own notification duties.
